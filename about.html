Our Artisanal Story

In every Indian wedding, the varmala exchange represents one of life's most sacred moments. Yet within days, these cherished flowers wilt, dry, and are often reluctantly discarded. Founded by polymer enthusiast and artisan Abhijit Kalita, Vistāra Krafts was born to solve this heartbreak.

What began in our Guwahati studio as bespoke floral preservation for friends and family quickly evolved into India's trusted artisanal keepsake studio and high-purity epoxy raw materials laboratory. Today, we have preserved floral memories for couples across 28 states and Union Territories, while supplying professional-grade resins to over 10,000+ crafters pan-India.

The 5-Stage Varmala Preservation Technique

Every bridal garland undergoes our proprietary laboratory preservation technique to prevent floral decay, yellowing, or structural collapse:

1

Botanical Desiccant Extraction

Fresh petals are immersed in optical silica crystal matrices, dehydrating moisture at cellular levels over 14 days while retaining natural pigments.

2

Cellular Priming & Pre-Sealing

Delicate dried blooms receive an ultra-thin barrier coat, locking capillary airways and preventing air release during deep resin immersion.

3

Dual-Degassing Vacuum Chamber

Our formulations are pulled through negative pressure (-0.098 MPa) vacuum chambers, extracting 99.8% of micro-bubbles before pouring.

4

Multi-Layer Temperature Curing

Deep blocks (up to 50mm) are cast in progressive layer heights inside climate-stabilized cleanrooms, preventing thermal discoloration.

5

Diamond Hand-Polishing & HALS UV Glaze

Every block is hand-sanded across 9 progressive grits (up to 7000 grit) and buffed to a diamond mirror sheen with hydrophobic UV protection.
