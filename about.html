About Vistāra Krafts Atelier

Preserving life's sacred milestones in museum-grade optical crystal epoxy. Born out of deep artistic passion, Vistāra Krafts (@resin.art.memories) bridges fine chemistry with timeless artisanal preservation.

Our Artisanal Story

Founded in India, Vistāra Krafts began with a singular promise: wedding varmalas, anniversary blooms, and sacred relics deserve to last a lifetime without yellowing, wilting, or decomposing. What started as bespoke botanical resin casting quickly grew into India's trusted source for both custom keepsake artistry and lab-tested DIY epoxy formulations.

Every keepsake is hand-cast by our master artisans through a proprietary 5-stage preservation technique: desiccant crystallization, cellular priming, dual-degassing vacuum treatment, slow-temperature exothermic curing, and diamond-edge hand polishing.
