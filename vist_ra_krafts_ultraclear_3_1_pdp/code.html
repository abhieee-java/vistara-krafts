Artisan Workshop Guides

tune 1. Digital Weighing & Mix Protocol expand_more

Always weigh components using a digital gram balance rather than cups. For every 300g of Part A (Resin), add exactly 100g of Part B (Hardener).

Mix slowly and steadily along the beaker wall for 3 to 4 continuous minutes. Scrape bottom corners twice to ensure no unreacted amine residues remain.

bubble_chart 2. Auto-Degassing & Thermal Release expand_more

UltraClear 3:1 is formulated with active air-release agents. Once blended, allow the beaker to stand untouched for 8–10 minutes before pouring; micro-bubbles will naturally rise and pop spontaneously.

A brief 1-second pass with a butane torch or isopropyl alcohol mist eliminates remaining surface meniscus tension.

health_and_safety 3. Studio Safety & VOC Footprint expand_more

Zero VOC formula emitting negligible chemical odor. Despite the gentle profile, work in a ventilated craft space, wear nitrile gloves, and use splash goggles.

Do not cast depths greater than 20mm in a single pass to prevent exothermic thermal acceleration.
