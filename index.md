## About Vistāra Krafts

Vistāra Krafts is Northeast India's premier artisanal resin art atelier and certified supplier of high-performance optical epoxy resin systems, precision silicone molds, and resin crafting raw materials. We specialize in custom bridal varmala and floral memory preservation keepsakes, capturing life's most cherished moments in crystal-clear, UV-stabilized archival epoxy blocks, wall clocks, and personalized mantels.

## Standard ISO-9001 Studio Mixing Procedure

1. **Calculate Gram Weights:** Use an accurate digital gram scale with a 2:1 or 3:1 weight ratio.
2. **Slow-Fold Mix for 3 to 4 Minutes:** Combine Part B into Part A and stir slowly for 3–4 minutes, scraping sidewalls and bottom of the cup until optical striations disappear.
3. **Pour & Cure:** Pour in a steady stream from the lowest corner of the mold, pass a brief heat torch over the surface to release micro-bubbles, and cure in a dust-free environment (24h–72h).
