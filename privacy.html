3. Google User Data & OAuth 2.0 Compliance Notice

When you select "Continue with Google", Vistāra Krafts requests access only to basic Google profile information via the standard OpenID Connect scopes (openid, email, profile).

  • Data Accessed: Your primary Google account email address, full name, and avatar profile picture.
  • Purpose of Access: To seamlessly authenticate your identity, create your Vistāra Krafts patron profile, and securely associate your orders and curated wishlist across devices without needing a separate password.
  • Data Sharing & Transfer: Vistāra Krafts NEVER sells, licenses, or transfers Google user data to third parties. We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing profiling, or AI training.
  • Limited Use Requirements: Vistāra Krafts' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

shield 4. Database Security & Order Isolation

Our PostgreSQL database implements strict Row Level Security (RLS). Customer orders, delivery addresses, and payment logs are cryptographically bound to the authenticated patron's unique user ID (auth.uid() = user_id). Customer A can never view, query, modify, or infer any order or address data belonging to Customer B under any network circumstance.

delete_sweep 5. Data Retention & User Deletion Requests

We retain your account profile and order records only for as long as necessary to fulfill purchases, provide customer warranty support, and comply with statutory tax laws (such as GST audit retention).

You may request complete erasure of your personal data and account at any time by:

  1. Sending an email from your registered account to grievance@vistarakrafts.com or support@vistarakrafts.com with the subject line "Account Data Erasure Request".
  2. Or opening the DPDP Grievance & Rights Portal from the website footer to submit a verified rights request.

Upon verification, your profile, authentication records, and uploaded media will be permanently deleted from our primary servers within 7 business days.
