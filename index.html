Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Notice 2023

🌐 Notice Language / भाषा:

This Notice is issued pursuant to Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 by Vistāra Krafts Studio & Chemical Atelier (@resin.art.memories) ("Data Fiduciary") before processing any personal data of Data Principals in India.

checklist 1. Itemized Personal Data Categories & Specified Purposes

📦 Recipient Identity & Delivery Address Full Name, Mobile/WhatsApp, Street Address, City, PIN Code for BlueDart air shipping, delivery SMS/WhatsApp alerts, and GST tax invoicing.
💐 Custom Bridal Keepsake Floral Photos Garland, bouquet, and keepsake layout photo uploads strictly for custom 50mm resin casting design, lettering proofs, and approval.
🔐 Auth & Account State Email address and 1-click Google/Apple auth tokens for account login, wishlist synchronization, and order history tracking.
💌 Atelier Circle Promotional Notes (Optional) Email address strictly upon explicit opt-in for discount code KRAFTFIRST and bridal slot release alerts.

verified_user 2. Your Statutory Rights under DPDP Act 2023

  • Right to Access Summary: Request an itemized export of all personal data held by Vistāra Krafts.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: Request correction or update of inaccurate contact/address details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request instant deletion of account profile and uploaded floral media (subject to statutory tax retention requirements).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw optional marketing or session consents at any time.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit formal grievances to our Data Protection Officer with a binding 7-day resolution SLA.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity.
