## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the material honesty, calm poise, and tactile precision of studio pottery and refined homeware. It balances high-end gallery editoriality with frictionless, conversion-focused commerce.

- **Brand Personality**: Tactile, architectural, warm, unhurried, and grounded. The interface behaves like an exhibition catalog—quietly framing the product rather than competing with it.
- **Target Audience**: Discerning interior design enthusiasts, collectors of handmade craft, culinary professionals, and gift buyers seeking enduring, functional art.
- **Emotional Response**: Elicits a sense of grounded calm, tactile reassurance, provenance, and absolute commercial trust.
- **Design Style**: Warm Minimalist Editorial. It relies on subtle 1px structural framing, rich ceramic and mineral tones, deliberate negative space, and disciplined typographic rhythm rather than synthetic effects, drop shadows, or heavy elevation blurs.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the pottery studio: raw clay, turned earth, drying slip, and mineral glazes. All pairings strictly conform to WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - `Surface Canvas`: Warm Ivory (`#F8F5EF`), establishing an organic, non-sterile baseline.
  - `Surface Raised / Card`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), offering crisp contrast for photography and interactive cards.
  - `Surface Subdued`: Sand Tonal (`#F1EBE1`), for secondary callouts, banners, and summary panels.

- **Typography & Structure**:
  - `Text Primary / Charcoal`: Deep Charcoal (`#24231F`), ensuring an accessible 13.8:1 contrast on ivory.
  - `Text Muted`: Weathered Umber (`#635F57`), for subtitles, captions, and secondary metadata (4.8:1 contrast).
  - `Border / Hairline`: Muted Sand (`#E7E0D6`), providing structured, quiet 1px separation.

- **Accents & Status**:
  - `Accent Clay`: Terracotta (`#B86F50`), used for calls to action, badges, and focal interactions.
  - `Accent Earth`: Muted Sage (`#78816C`), indicating collections, natural materials, and sustainable provenance.
  - `In-Stock / Positive`: Deep Forest Green (`#2D5A27`), delivering clear inventory status with high readability against ivory.
  - `Alert / Error`: Terra Rust (`#A63A24`), for validation warnings and critical stock scarcity indicators.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs an editorial high-contrast serif with an open, humanist sans-serif.

- **Playfair Display** delivers refined craft, editorial rhythm, and curatorial dignity. It is reserved exclusively for major headlines, collection titles, and curated editorial interludes. Never apply faux-bolding; let the optical weight express itself at `400` and `500`.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** grounds the storefront with utilitarian clarity. It powers commerce interactions: prices, technical dimension tables, glaze specifications, cart tallies, and inventory statuses.
- **Case Rules**: Section pre-headers, micro-tags, and artisanal dimension tags make measured use of `label-caps` (all-caps with `0.08em` tracking) to structure content like museum curation markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured 12-column responsive grid framed by architectural hairline dividers.

- **Desktop (12 Columns, max-width 1440px)**: 48px outer canvas margin, 24px column gutters. Editorial hero modules and collection introductions span 8 or 12 columns, while product catalogue sections utilize a 3-column (4 products per row) or 4-column (3 products per row) arrangement.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1024px)**: 32px margins, 16px gutters. Catalogue displays collapse to 2 columns with alternating asymmetric aspect ratios to preserve the gallery aesthetic.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px)**: 20px margins, 16px gutters. Product listings shift to a clean 1-column high-detail stack or a structured 2-column compact grid.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Element gaps respect an 8px base grid. Spacing is intentionally generous around imagery (`space-xl`), allowing ceramic forms breathing room against ivory backdrops.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects arbitrary shadows, floating blur filters, and multi-tiered z-axis elevation. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering** and **architectural 1px framing**.

- **The Baseline Plane**: Warm Ivory (`#F8F5EF`) acts as the natural surface level for the entire application canvas.
- **Framed Inset**: Product cards, modal dialogs, and drawer sheets are articulated through razor-thin borders (`1px solid #E7E0D6`) over Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces.
- **Subtle Ambient Grounding**: When modals or sliding cart sheets overlay the viewport, use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with warm charcoal: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(36, 35, 31, 0.08)`. Avoid stark black drop shadows.
- **Active State Depth**: Interactive state changes are expressed through tactile shifts in background tone (e.g., Warm Ivory transitioning to Muted Sand `#F1EBE1`) rather than artificial elevation lifting.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, structured architectural discipline (`0.5rem` / `8px` base border radius).

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Applied to interactive buttons, text inputs, variant selectors, and product image containers. This imparts a tailored, hand-finished ceramic edge rather than an overly digital or severe 90-degree corner.
- **Chips & Pills**: Hyperlocal delivery chips, material provenance badges, and glaze swatches utilize a constrained `0.5rem` or full circular geometry for swatch discs, avoiding generic hyper-rounded UI pills that undermine high-end retail credibility.
- **Divider Hairlines**: 1px crisp horizontal and vertical rules that seamlessly integrate with element boundaries to evoke architectural shelving and grid-based vitrines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Charcoal (`#24231F`) fill, Pure White text, `0.5rem` radius, 48px height. On hover: Terracotta (`#B86F50`) fill with immediate tactile transition.
- **Secondary**: Transparent background, 1px solid Deep Charcoal (`#24231F`), Deep Charcoal text. On hover: filled `#F1EBE1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, underlined on hover, 1px Muted Sand divider baseline.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Encapsulated within a pure white canvas or subtle 1px `#E7E0D6` frame.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 vertical framing optimized for vessels, bowls, and tabletop arrangements.
- **Details**: Unobtrusive top-right heart icon (1.5px stroke, no solid circle backing). Title set in `Playfair Display`, pricing in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold. Bottom strip houses inline ceramic glaze swatch chips (16px circles with a 1px boundary ring).

### Hyperlocal Delivery Chip & Postal Selector
- Compact inline component rendered at the top of the product display page and in the cart drawer.
- Styled as a 1px `#E7E0D6` bordered segment with `#F8F5EF` backing, featuring a pinpoint glyph, delivery readiness indicator ("Ready for dispatch within 24h"), and an interactive postal code trigger with subtle underline.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs**: 48px height, Pure White background, 1px solid `#E7E0D6` border, `Plus Jakarta Sans` 15px text. Focus state transitions border to Deep Charcoal (`#24231F`) with a zero-spread border outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px squares/circles, 1.5px `#635F57` stroke. Checked state fills with `#24231F` with a crisp white tick or inner dot.

### Variant & Glaze Chips
- Segmented rectangular chips for sizing (e.g., "Set of 4", "Single Vessel") with 1px hairline edges. Active state highlights with `#24231F` border and subtle `#F8F5EF` fill.
- Glaze selectors feature actual material photo swatches within 24px circles with a 2px offset border ring when selected.

### Inventory Status Badges
- Set with `label-caps`. In-stock uses Deep Forest Green (`#2D5A27`) paired with a 6px solid circular dot. Low stock/made-to-order leverages Terracotta (`#B86F50`).
