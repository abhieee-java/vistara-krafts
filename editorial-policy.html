Editorial Policy & Review Standards

Our editorial standards govern every resin tutorial, calculation formula, botanical preservation guide, and technical data sheet published across Vistāra Krafts (@resin.art.memories).

1. Technical Accuracy & Polymer Laboratory Verification

Polymer chemistry leaves zero margin for approximation. All mixing ratios (including 3:1 by weight for deep casting, 2:1 for high-gloss tableware, and 1:1 for surface doming), gel times, Shore D durometer ratings, and exothermic profiles published on our website undergo rigorous studio laboratory verification.

Because ambient humidity and temperature severely affect epoxy cross-linking, all formulation guidelines are stress-tested across Indian climate parameters ranging from 18°C in winter to 38°C in monsoon conditions (40%–85% Relative Humidity) at our Guwahati testing studio.

health_and_safety

2. Chemical Safety, Toxicity & PPE Protocol

Artist safety is paramount. Every technical guide and project walkthrough mandatorily specifies proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) standards:

  • Dermal Protection: Mandatory use of powder-free Nitrile gloves (latex is permeable to epoxy amine hardeners).
  • Respiratory Protection: Guidance on natural ventilation and certified organic-vapor dual-cartridge respirators during large exothermic pours.
  • Cured State Certification: Clear distinction between liquid resins (sensitizers requiring safety protocols) and fully cross-linked polymers (inert, food-contact compliant under ASTM D-4236 guidelines).
  • Eco-Responsible Disposal: Strict instructions never to pour liquid resins down drains; proper techniques for curing scrap resin before disposal.

Using optical-grade silica micro-crystals to extract 100% moisture while locking natural floral pigments.

4. Unbiased Reviews & Commercial Independence

Vistāra Krafts maintains uncompromising integrity across all patron and crafter communications:

  • 100% Verified Buyer Reviews: We never purchase, incentivize, or manufacture fake reviews. Every review badge is cryptographically linked to genuine order IDs.
  • Transparent Limitations: We openly disclose product boundaries, such as maximum single-pour thickness (e.g. 15mm for 2:1 vs 50mm for 3:1) to prevent customer project failures.
  • Zero Third-Party Ad Placement: We host zero sponsored advertorials or affiliate ad networks.
