Editorial Policy & Review Process

Our editorial standards govern every tutorial, guide, mixing ratio calculator, and craft instruction published across Vistāra Krafts channels.

1. Technical Accuracy & Lab Verification

Polymer chemistry requires exact calculations. Every epoxy ratio (e.g. 2:1 by weight vs volume, 3:1 deep pour limits), cure schedule, and exotherm limit published on our site is lab-verified by certified resin specialists. We test formulations across varying humidity and ambient temperatures (18°C–38°C) typical of Indian climates.

2. Chemical Safety & PPE Guidelines

Crafter safety is non-negotiable. All technical content includes mandatory safety warnings: wearing nitrile gloves, working in well-ventilated studios or using organic vapor respirators, and keeping raw chemicals out of reach of children and pets.

3. Unbiased Reviews & Transparency

All customer reviews displayed on our catalogue reflect genuine verified purchases. We never incentivize fabricated ratings or manipulate feedback. When describing product limitations (such as maximum single-pour depth), we provide explicit, transparent technical warnings.

4. Corrections & Updates Policy

If any instruction, formula, or specification contains an inaccuracy, our editorial team promptly investigates and publishes clear corrections with revision timestamps. Inquiries or corrections can be reported directly to our technical desk at studio@vistarakrafts.com.
